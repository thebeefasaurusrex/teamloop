# TeamLoop brand kit v1.0

## Locked core

Assembly Cinch / AC1 refined-junction symbol. Anta Regular 400 wordmark, written TeamLoop. Arcade blue #315BFF for the symbol and Arcade green #43F052 for the wordmark. Plain on dark navy #0C1220; navy outline plus a tight, zero-blur hard shadow on light backgrounds. Do not change the fills to suit the background.

The approved light treatment uses a 0.045em outline with paint-order: stroke fill and a 0.045em right / 0.05em down navy shadow, blur zero. It is not outline alone. A dark backing is a utility fallback for small/busy placements or incompatible software, not the normal light treatment.

## Supporting system

Sora 400 is the recommended reading font; Sora 600 adds emphasis. This is a companion recommendation, not a new locked wordmark. Anta stays at native 400. No fake bold, italic, stretch or extra tracking. Navy/off-white provide ordinary reading text. No orange, StarForge purple/magenta/cyan mix, gradients or glow.

Working spacing rule: keep at least one-quarter of the symbol height clear around a lockup. Start at a 24px wordmark and 52px-wide symbol for compact use, then inspect the real destination. These are conservative working guidelines, not validated universal minima. The detailed symbol is not guaranteed at 16px favicon size.

## Assets and use

assets/ contains horizontal and stacked lockups for light/dark destinations, a blue symbol, and an avatar. PNG exports have transparent grounds except the navy avatar tile. SVG files embed the approved raster symbol and Anta font. They are SVG containers, not fully vector production masters. The display mask removes near-white paper texture and makes near-black ink opaque using a symmetric linear contrast clamp (1.25 slope, -0.125 intercept after inversion). The source pixels and geometry are not edited or retraced. Use PNG in programs that misrender SVG filters, embedded fonts or text shadows. Do not retrace or regenerate the logo without permission. Large-format print, embroidery, one-color reproduction and tiny favicon design remain outside this digital kit.

fonts/ includes the approved Anta webfont, a desktop Anta TTF from its recorded source commit, and a Sora variable TTF. Font licenses are in licenses/. css/teamloop.css uses relative font paths. design-tokens.json holds portable roles and values. AI-HANDOFF.md holds all seven copy-ready sections from the end of the visual guide.

## Product description and voice

Created by Mark Salisbury with Codex, inspired by Claudex Loop and implemented separately. It is not a new model, hosted chat app, universal leaderboard or guaranteed savings claim.

Direct, curious, practical and a little playful. Explain plainly before technical detail. Do not force jokes, invent metrics or imply provider endorsement. No em dashes. Optional descriptor: Many models. One conversation. This descriptor is supporting copy, not text baked into the mark.
